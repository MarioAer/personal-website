## 1. Purpose

A personal website that presents Mario Erazo, a cloud architect, and his work in solution architecture, engineering leadership and AI-assisted engineering. The site is not a CV. It states which problems Mario solves, shows evidence, and makes contact easy.

The same specification is implemented by several AI models, and the visitor can switch between the implementations with a "Built with" selector in the top bar. The specification itself is readable from the site. The mechanic is a demonstration of the third area of expertise (AI-assisted engineering): the same specification, several agents, comparable results.

### 3.4 How I work

Seven principles. Each has a title and a text of one to three sentences, at most 50 words. The text is written in the first person. Variants present the principles as a list or as short paragraphs.

1. **Context before decisions.** I gather the business and technical context first, investigate the options and weigh their trade-offs. I write an RFC after consulting the developers who will build the solution, and record the outcome in an architecture decision record.
2. **Leadership as peers.** I lead as a peer, support engineers in doing their best work, and keep work progressing. I change my position when a different opinion rests on a real problem that the other person has understood.
3. **Development that serves the person and the work.** Delegation is part of planning. The development of each engineer follows two inputs: the engineer's own goals, and what the current requirements need.
4. **Shared product over bespoke forks.** A client requirement first passes a business analysis of its relevance. A relevant requirement is generalised into a product feature. A requirement that cannot be generalised is funded by the client and released behind a feature flag for that client only.
5. **Fewer components.** Every service, database and pipeline adds operating cost and cognitive load. I treat consolidation as a deliverable and plan it like any other feature.
6. **Context in the repository.** Architecture notes and agent instructions live in each repository and, where needed, in each module. Engineers and agents see what a change affects before they make it, which keeps changes targeted.
7. **Measured outcomes.** I judge a change by its measured effect on cost, lead time and release cadence, compared before and after the change.

### 4.2 Content rules

- Clients are described, not named: "four airlines and two airports", "a major European airport", "a Nordic department store group", "an Austrian logistics group". Employers may be named.
- Contact data is limited to LinkedIn and GitHub. No email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, photograph or nationality. The professional facts in 4.1 are permitted.
- Numbers appear exactly as written in this document. A variant that cannot fit a number omits it.
- Language: English. Formal register, no contractions, no idioms, no emojis.
- Variants may write their own headlines and connective copy, but every factual statement must trace to sections 3 and 4.
